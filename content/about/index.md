I'm Nick Herrig. I like building things. I work with customers on computer vision at Roboflow.

I'm drawn to startups because I like moving fast and learning as I build. In a high-growth company, the problems keep changing, and I enjoy figuring them out with other people.

I want to help build technology that changes how people work and live. I care about how it works, why someone needs it, and what it takes to get it into their hands.

## Projects and writing

Hunter, Nathan, and I built [Split the G](https://splittheg.dev/), a web app that uses computer vision to score a Guinness split. I've written about [how we built it](/posts/building-split-the-g/).

Outside of that, I like experimenting. I've built an [Obsidian plugin to clean up meeting notes](/posts/ollama-obsidian/), trained a [cocktail classifier](/posts/drink-classifier/), and built my own [standing desk](/posts/desk-build/).

I use this site to share those projects and write about what I'm learning. The blog also has some travel posts and other things I've been interested in.

## Speaking

I moderated a robotics panel at Roboflow’s Visual Intelligence Summit with BOPA, Medra, and Standard Bots. We talked about deploying robots in farms, labs, and factories. [Watch the panel and read my notes](/posts/vis-2026/).
